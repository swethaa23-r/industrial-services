Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can your emergency support team respond?

For contracted partners, our emergency technical support teams guarantee a remote response within 15 minutes and on-site deployment within 4 hours depending on the facility's location.

Do you handle international industrial projects?

Yes. We have successfully executed engineering and automation projects across 15+ countries. Our global logistics team ensures compliance with local industrial regulations and standards.

Are your engineers certified for high-voltage environments?

Absolutely. All our electrical engineers hold advanced certifications for high-voltage systems and adhere strictly to global safety protocols including OSHA and IEC standards.

Yes, retrofitting legacy systems is one of our core specialties. We design custom PLCs, sensors, and IoT gateways to bring modern data collection and automation to older heavy machinery.
